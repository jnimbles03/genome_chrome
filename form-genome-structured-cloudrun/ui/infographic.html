Form Analyzer Indicators & Complexity Scoring

Understanding the signals we extract from forms and how we score their difficulty

Overview & Simplified Formulas

This infographic summarises the core signals extracted from forms and explains how two key scores are computed.

  • Complexity Score: Measures the structural and cognitive load of a form. Factors include page count, field count, signatures (including witness and notary requirements), attachments, conditional logic, unique signer and third‑party roles, PII collection, drawings and images per page, deadlines and dependencies, and XFA hints. Each factor contributes a weighted number of points, and the sum is capped at 100. (Domain mismatch weighting is not currently implemented in the scoring logic.)
  • NIGO Risk Score: Estimates the likelihood that a form will be returned not in good order. It is calculated as 50% of the complexity score plus surcharges for common risk factors: +20 points if attachments are required, +15 points for notarization requirements, and +10 points when conditional logic is present. The total is capped at 100.

Key Indicators & Their Meanings

View detailed indicator descriptions

Required Fields (REQ‑001)

Identifies fields that should be mandatory (e.g., SSN, DOB, account details) but are marked as optional. Missing required markings raises a high NIGO risk.

Severity: High

Input Masks (FMT‑001)

Detects high‑risk data fields lacking validation (no input mask or formatting checks). This includes SSN, routing or account numbers, and phone numbers.

Severity: Medium

Signature Date Missing (SIG‑002)

Warns when a signature line lacks an adjacent date field. Missing dates can delay processing and trigger follow‑up.

Severity: Low

Conditional Logic Missing (COND‑003)

Marks sections referencing beneficiaries or dependents without visible logic (e.g., JavaScript) to control follow‑on questions. Suggests adding data masks for SSN/DOB.

Severity: Medium

Role Unspecified (ROLE‑001)

Flags signature fields without a labelled role (Applicant, Co‑owner, Notary, etc.). Adding role labels reduces confusion and NIGO risk.

Severity: Medium

Third‑Party Coordination (THRD‑001)

Highlights when additional parties (Physician, Attorney, Financial Advisor, Guardian, etc.) must sign or approve the form. Coordination increases complexity.

Severity: Medium

Attachment Requirements

Looks for text like “attach,” “include copy,” or mentions of supporting documents. Required attachments add to overall complexity and NIGO risk.

Signatures & Notarization

Counts signature fields and identifies witness and notary requirements. Witness signatures and notarization materially increase form difficulty.

JavaScript / Conditional Logic

Detects embedded JavaScript or multistep logic. Complex logic (branching questions, dynamic sections) signals high cognitive load for signers.

Radio Groups

Counts sets of radio buttons. Multiple radio groups indicate branching choices that may confuse signers and increase complexity.

PII Collection

Scans for sensitive personal data (SSN, DOB, TIN, EIN, account and routing numbers, address, email, phone). Each type of PII adds to the complexity score.

Graphics & Layout

Measures drawings and images per page and text density. Many diagrams or busy pages increase cognitive load and the complexity score.

Deadlines & Dependencies

Extracts deadline mentions (“submit within 30 days”) and references to other forms (dependencies). Each deadline or dependency slightly raises complexity.

Domain Mismatch

Checks whether the hosting domain differs from the issuer/brand. Mismatched domains can signal phishing or confusion and add modest complexity.

NIGO Risk Score Formula

The NIGO (Not In Good Order) risk score estimates the likelihood that a submitted form will be returned for corrections. It is calculated as follows:

  • Base component: 50% of the form’s complexity score.
  • Attachments surcharge: +20 points if the form requires supporting documents or attachments.
  • Notarization surcharge: +15 points if the form must be notarized or includes notary language.
  • Conditional logic surcharge: +10 points if the form contains JavaScript or other conditional/branching logic.

The surcharges are added to the base component and the total is capped at 100. A higher NIGO score indicates a greater risk of a form being rejected or delayed because it is not in good order.
